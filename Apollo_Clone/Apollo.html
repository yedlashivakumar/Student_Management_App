FAQ's

Does EasyCheck - Breast™ detect 'existence of cancer' or 'possibility of cancer'?
+
EasyCheck - Breast™ is designed to detect the presence of specific biomarkers associated with breast cancer. The test provides an indication of cancer risk based on these markers.
Does EasyCheck - Breast™ replace conventional cancer screening like mammography?
+
EasyCheck - Breast™ is not intended to be and should not be considered a replacement for any Standard of Care screening tests.
Does a 'negative' EasyCheck - Breast™ result mean that I am 'totally' cancer free?
+
No. A negative result indicates no detected biomarkers associated with breast cancer at the time of testing, but it does not guarantee the complete absence of cancer.
How frequently should EasyCheck - Breast™ be repeated?
+
It is recommended to consult your doctor for personalized screening intervals based on your medical history and risk factors.
